FloUV Value Proposition and Leadership

FloUV's superior microbial disinfection delivers validated, non-thermal UV-C inactivation of vegetative cells, pathogens, yeast, molds, viruses, and heat-resistant spores, meeting FDA HACCP expectations with a 5-7 log pathogen reduction, without heat or chemicals.

Gentle preservation means no heat, no pressure, no chemicals: natural taste, nutrition, and functionality are preserved. Heat-sensitive bioactives such as vitamins, polyphenols, and amino acids are protected, and native flavor and aroma are retained.

FloUV runs as a continuous, inline flow-through system rather than a batch process, reaching close to 100% yield with no rejection, since liquid is treated directly with no membranes, hold tanks, or batching required.

Dr. Ankit Patras is FloUV's Chief Technology Officer. He pioneered the light-matter interaction and fluid-dynamics breakthrough behind FloUV, overcoming the fundamental limits of conventional UV treatment in opaque, viscous liquids, and leads FloUV's UV-C engineering and scientific validation work.
